Generated by `scripts/make_figures_results.py` from `results/m3/*.json` (written by `scripts/m3_report.py`). Files: `figures/results_*.pdf` and `.png`. Single-column width (3.5 in). Palette: Okabe-Ito (blue = FedQPNT, vermillion = undefended, green = Baseline A, orange = B-cont/second grade, pink = FedQPNT without CAI). Any bar with fewer than 30 seeds carries an `n=k` tag; renders made while the campaign is incomplete are provisional. Captions describe what is plotted, not the outcome; the Master/author adds the interpretation.

**Fig. R1 — `results_defended_vs_undefended`.** Attack-window horizontal RMSE (m, log scale) of FedQPNT, the undefended filter and Baseline A for each attack scenario (S2 gradual spoof at three severities, S2-DM displaced meaconer, S3 jamming, S4 jam-then-spoof capture, S6 Schuler-world gradual spoof) at the industrial-MEMS (top) and tactical (bottom) IMU grades. Bars are means over the test seeds 10000-10029; error bars are 95% t-intervals of the mean. (S7 toggled-spoof scenarios are omitted from this figure and shown in the safety overview.)

**Fig. R2 — `results_cai_coasting`.** Exploratory (S6-coast, not in the confirmatory family). Maximum horizontal coasting error (m) during a 180 s forced GNSS outage with and without the cold-atom interferometer (CAI), per IMU grade; mean over seeds with 95% t-interval.

**Fig. R3 — `results_timing_meaconing`.** Exploratory. Clock-estimate RMSE (ns) under the displaced meaconer (S2-DM) for FedQPNT, Baseline A and the undefended filter, per IMU grade; mean over seeds with 95% t-interval.

**Fig. R4 — trust-weight trajectories: NOT PRODUCED.** The M4 result files hold scalar metrics only (`RunSpec.record = False`; no per-epoch trust trace is stored for the single-node attack scenarios), so no representative-seed trajectories can be drawn from the test-seed data without re-running (not permitted: test seeds are run once). Revisit only if the Master authorises a recorded re-run of one seed per attack.

**Fig. R5 — `results_h2h4_auc_paired`.** Per-seed onset AUC (first 10 post-onset epochs, N = 10) for the FL arm (FedQPNT) and the continuous centralised-training baseline (B-cont), paired by seed, for the H2 abrupt-onset experiment and the H4 cold-start node (core-freeze-4, seeds 500-509; D-081). Grey lines join the two arms of one seed; the title gives the uncorrected paired Wilcoxon p-value. The pre-registered primaries (P_D@10 s and onset latency) are saturated at 1.0 and 0 s for every seed and arm and are therefore not plotted.

**Fig. R6 — `results_safety_overview`.** Safety principle: paired Hodges-Lehmann difference in attack-window maximum horizontal error (defended minus undefended; `max_h_pre` for the attack-free S1) per scenario and IMU grade, with 95% BCa bootstrap confidence intervals (symmetric-log axis; values left of zero favour the defended filter). The dotted line marks the pre-registered 3σ_nom margin (≈3.4 m) applied to the mean difference in the primary criterion. Scenario/grade cells with fewer than 30 seeds carry an `n=k` tag.
