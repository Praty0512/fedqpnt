# Dataset

## Third-party raw data (NOT included)
FedQPNT calibrates its quantum-sensor noise model on the published cold-atom interferometer shot data:

d'Armagnac de Castanet, Q.; Des Cognets, C.; Arguel, R.; Templier, S.; Jarlaud, V.; Menoret, V.; Desruelle, B.; Bouyer, P.; Battelier, B., "Atom interferometry at arbitrary orientations and rotation rates," Nat. Commun. 15, 6406 (2024). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-50804-0

- Dataset DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.11543715 (https://zenodo.org/records/11543715), licence CC-BY-4.0
- File: `Raw Data.zip`, 875,547,718 bytes, MD5 `103d382e42a6f600fd0768c2a3286c40`
- Download: `wget https://zenodo.org/records/11543715/files/Raw%20Data.zip`
- Verify: `echo "103d382e42a6f600fd0768c2a3286c40  Raw Data.zip" | md5sum -c -`
- Place: extract to `code/data/raw/jarlaud2024/` (so files sit under `data/raw/jarlaud2024/extracted/Raw Data/...`; ~2.9 GB unzipped; the raw data is ~3.6 GB on the author's disk including extraction).
  Contents: Figure 2 (rigid-mode contrast), Figure 3 & 5 (inertial-pointing mode, 56 runs), Figure 4 (classical accelerometer PSD).
- File manifest: `code/data/processed/jarlaud2024/manifest.csv` (also in `derived/processed_jarlaud2024/`), generated by `scripts/jarlaud2024_manifest.py`. Details: `code/docs/DATA.md`, `code/docs/REPRODUCE.md`.

The raw data is only needed to re-run the calibration scripts. The simulation campaign does not read it.

## Derived calibration outputs (included)
- `derived/processed_jarlaud2024/` : calibration.json (classical MICAL noise), atom_residual_summary.json, atom_outliers.json, per-shot residual .npz files, CSVs, manifest.csv
- `derived/results_data/` : calibration figures (Allan deviation, contrast vs rotation, streaming PSD)

## Simulation datasets
All simulation datasets (the 5,340 round-3 runs) are in `code/results/m4r3` as JSON, together with `code/results/minimatrix_f8`, `demo`, `m1`, `fleet`. Superseded rounds 1 and 2 are omitted.
